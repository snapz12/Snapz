Community Guidelines

Be Respectful

Treat other Slovzan users with respect. Do not harass, threaten, bully, or deliberately target other people.

Keep Slovzan Safe

Do not use Slovzan to promote dangerous activities, illegal activity, scams, or content intended to harm others.

Respect Privacy

Do not share another person's private information without their permission.

No Spam

Avoid repetitive unwanted messages, fake engagement, misleading links, and other spam-like behavior.

Authentic Content

Do not impersonate other people or create misleading accounts intended to deceive users.

Report Problems

If you see content or behavior that violates these guidelines, use the Report a Problem option so the Slovzan team can review it.

Enforcement

Slovzan may review reported content and take appropriate action when these guidelines are violated.
